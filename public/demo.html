Welcome

This is a simple demonstration page showing how the Staten Island Constituent Chatbot appears when it is added to a webpage. A small card pops up in the corner a moment after the page loads to say what it is. Tap the card to open the full chat, or dismiss it and it folds down to the chat button in the corner, where it stays for the rest of your visit.

Try asking it how to get a marriage license, when Borough Hall is open, or how to report litter on your street.
